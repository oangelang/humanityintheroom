Whatever we build, people belong in the room.

Humanity in the Room helps people and organizations use AI in ways that put humans first, through gatherings, education and hands-on advising. We work across climate, health, education and economic mobility.

The name

In “human in the loop,” a person only checks a machine’s work. We hold that people should help decide what gets built, who it is for and who it might harm.

AI is a tool. Human wellbeing and development are the goal. We keep both in view.

Climate

Does AI help the climate or hurt it?

What AI can do

AI can help communities forecast floods, cut energy waste, and track a changing planet.

What we watch for

AI also uses real water and power, and the costs land on real places. We ask who pays.

Health

Will AI make care better, or leave people out?

What AI can do

AI can stretch scarce clinicians, catch disease earlier, and reach patients far from a hospital.

What we watch for

It can also carry bias, leak private data, and stand in for human care where people need it most.

Education

Does AI help people learn, or stop them thinking?

What AI can do

AI can tutor, translate, and open doors for learners who never had one.

What we watch for

It can also let people stop thinking for themselves. We teach people to use it without losing that.

Economic mobility

Does AI open doors or close them?

What AI can do

AI can help someone start a business, write a résumé, or get through a benefits system.

What we watch for

It can also hollow out entry-level work and widen the gap for people already behind.

What we do

Gather.

Events where builders, nonprofits, funders and others talk.

Teach.

Practical, honest AI education, including ethics and risks. We train teams to build their own tools and manage their own data, so the skills stay when we leave.

Advise.

Hands-on support for organizations.

Who it’s for

Nonprofit leaders. Foundation and institutional decision-makers who worry about AI risk and want to talk to people who study it. Tech workers moving into tech for good. Anyone who wants AI to serve people.

Nine events so far. More than 2,000 people in the community.
