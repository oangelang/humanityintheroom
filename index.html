Whatever we build, people belong in the room.

Humanity in the Room helps people and organizations use AI in ways that put humans first, through gatherings, education, and hands-on advising.

Email us Join the community

The name

In AI, "human in the loop" means a person checks the machine's work. We think people deserve more than that. They should be in the room when the decisions get made: what gets built, who it's for, and who it might hurt.

What we do

Gather.

Rooms where builders, nonprofits, funders, and the curious actually talk to each other.

Teach.

Practical, honest AI education, including the ethics and the risks.

Advise.

Hands-on support for organizations figuring out how AI fits their mission.
